## 2. LinkedIn Post (3 users)
Post Day 28 + Comment: "Comment 'TRY' and I'll send you the demo link"
Reply to every comment in <10 mins - algorithm loves it.

## 3. Friends / Family (2 users)
Ask 2 non-tech friends: "Can you try my bot? Tell me what confused you?"
Non-tech feedback = Best UI feedback. If your mom can't use it, recruiter can't either.

## 4. Fix Loop (Same Day)
- Collect feedback in feedback_log.json
- Fix TOP 1 bug only (don't rebuild everything)
- Post fix on LinkedIn: "You said X was confusing. I fixed it. Try now."
- This is Build In Public - people love seeing you listen.
